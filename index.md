## English Version

Welcome to "AmiGo - Trip" (referred to as "this Application", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information in compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines 1.2 and 5.1.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage Principles
* **Account-Free Experience:** You can use all core itinerary planning features without creating an account. Your nickname and avatar color are saved locally on your device.
* **Itinerary and Expense Data:** Your travel spots, transit, lodging, and split-expense details are stored locally by default in the on-device SwiftData database.
* **Cloud Backup and Collaboration Sync:**
  * If you enable Apple iCloud backup, your data is securely synchronized through Apple's native iCloud service; our team cannot access your private iCloud storage.
  * If you create or join a shared collaborative trip, itinerary data and member names are synchronized in real-time via secure Firebase / CloudKit cloud infrastructure.
* **Location Services:** When granted location permissions, location data is only used locally to calculate spot distances and display map navigation. We do not track or log your location in the background.

### 2. User-Generated Content (UGC) & Community Safety Policy
In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 1.2, this Application enforces a **Zero-Tolerance Policy** against objectionable, abusive, or harassing content:
1. **Prohibited Content:** Users may not post, upload, or share any objectionable, pornographic, hateful, defamatory, violent, threatening, copyright-infringing, or fraudulent content.
2. **Reporting Mechanism:** Users can flag inappropriate itineraries, content, or members directly within the app using the in-app "Report" form.
3. **24-Hour Action Guarantee:** We review all reports within **24 hours**. Violating content will be removed immediately and offending accounts/users will be blocked.
4. **Immediate Blocking Mechanism:** Users can tap "Block this user" on any collaborative trip or member at any time. The blocked content/member disappears immediately from the local device and is added to the block list.

### 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services
* **No Sale of Personal Data:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data to any advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.
* **Trusted Infrastructure Partners:**
  * **Apple Inc. (iCloud & StoreKit):** Manages in-app subscriptions and private iCloud storage according to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  * **Google Firebase:** Powers secure, encrypted real-time collaborative syncing for shared trips.
